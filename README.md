A city pigeon pitches investors its startup — in one line, 172 characters, no wasted crumbs.

## The pitch

> Investors, meet CrumbDash: my 40,000 pigeons deliver your leftovers before the rats do — no drivers, no rent, just wings, a statue to nap on, and a seed round. Actual seed.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib-only, deterministic) writes the pitch to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`. No network, no secrets, no dependencies — just one very confident pigeon.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```
